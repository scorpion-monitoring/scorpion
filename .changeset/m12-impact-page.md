The impact page: Reviewers and Admin (`kpi.impact.read`) open **Impact** in the navigation, at `/services/evaluate`, choose a consortium and a year, and read how many of its services there are, how many are open source (with the licence lines that add up to the total), how many are in each stage (the labels of the vocabulary, deactivated stages included) and the yearly table of one headline KPI per service, with a state in words and what to do about it. A switch counts the unpublished services too. The figures are computed when the page is opened and each time a choice changes, with each service's current headline for every year. The page is in English and German, works with the keyboard and a screen reader, and uses no colour alone to say a state; it is part of the `full` profile only (the module has no tables and no job; the stored model was dropped). The table route gains the field `shown` (the figures written for reading: a duration is `HH:MM:SS`), an optional `display` on the entries of the registry `kpi.valueType`, and the seeded terms of the vocabulary `aggregate` get German labels on a new instance (an existing instance keeps its labels and can add them in the vocabulary editor). The public API is unchanged.
