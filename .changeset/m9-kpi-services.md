M9 sprint 3: services have a KPI set. A service can name one KPI set and any number of extra indicators; its effective KPIs are the set's indicators with their necessity plus the extras (which count as optional). Two tables (`kpif_service_set`, `kpif_service_extra_indicator`) are created at start. The create wizard of the `full` profile has a fourth and last step, "KPIs": choose a set, see its indicators, add extras; the service and its KPIs are created in one transaction or not at all. The service page has a section "KPIs" that the managers of the service's provider and administrators can change (plain members of the provider can read it) (set and extras are replaced in one request; removing an extra works). New internal routes: `GET` and `PUT /kpi/services/{id}/kpis` (new scoped permission `kpi.framework.service.kpis.edit`, held by Admin and the approved managers of the provider). The catalogue has a facet and a filter "KPI set" (`?kpi-set=`, and "No KPI set"). A KPI set or indicator that a service uses can no longer be deleted (409 naming the count). Public API v1, additions only: `GET /api/v1/categories` and `GET /api/v1/indicators?category=&service=` (new scope `kpi.framework.v1.read`, granted to the role `user` by default; the legacy fields plus `id`, `iri`, `unit`, bounds and more; `totalCount` follows the filters), and the service row of `GET /api/v1/services` gains `category` (the name of the service's KPI set) with a `category` filter. The audit trail records `kpi.serviceSet.changed@1`. For module authors: the `ui` entry of a module may export `wizardExtensions`, a field type may use the storage `module` with a `store` function, and wizard fields and steps may carry a `showIf` condition (see ADR-0040).
