People can ask to join an organisation, and organisation managers decide on their own organisation's requests. This is the first time a right belongs to people inside one organisation.

For API users: new internal routes. `POST` and `DELETE /organisations/{id}/membership` ask to join, withdraw or leave (any signed-in person; `201` for a new or reopened request, `200` when already asked or a member; `422 membership-not-supported` for a type without members; `409 too-many-pending`); `GET /account/memberships` lists your own. `GET /organisations/{id}/members` lists the approved members (usernames and join dates only, never an address or a state). `GET /memberships` (requests and members the caller may decide on, oldest first, with the actions each row allows), `GET /memberships/summary` (`pending` and `manages`, zeros for a plain user), `POST /memberships/{id}/decision` (`approved` or `rejected`), `POST /memberships/{id}/role` (`member` or `manager`) and `POST /memberships/{id}/remove` are for administrators and the managers of that organisation, and answer `403` to everybody else. Nobody approves their own request or changes their own role, and nobody removes themselves (use leave); a manager removes plain members only, never another manager. `GET /organisations` and `GET /organisations/{id}` now show `memberCount` (it was always 0) and `myMembership` (your own row, or `null`). A second decider who arrives late gets `409 membership-state`.

For administrators: an organisation manager can decide membership requests, promote and demote members, remove plain members and see the organisation's contact point, for that one organisation. An Admin can do all of that for every organisation, and alone removes a manager. Nobody can approve their own request, not even an Admin. A manager's rights follow the database at every call: a demotion or removal takes effect at once. The last manager may leave; the organisation then falls back to the administrators, who get an inbox item. Three new settings in `registry.organisations`: `membership.maxPendingPerUser` (default 10), `membership.maxManagersPerOrganisation` (default 20) and `membership.membersVisibleToMembers` (default on). New permissions: `registry.organisations.membership.request` (role User; Admin holds all) and the scoped `membership.view-members`, `membership.decide`, `membership.manage-roles` and `membership.remove`; the scoped `organisation.read-contact` is now held by managers too. The role User gets `membership.request` by default; an account that lacks it cannot ask. `core.audit` logs a decision, a role change and a membership ended by an Admin or a manager as security-critical events. One migration (`org_membership`); a deleted organisation takes its memberships with it, and a purged account's memberships are deleted.

For operators: the two membership mails moved from `core.notifications` into `registry.organisations` with the same template keys and are worded for organisations ("organisation" instead of "provider"; the data fields are `organisations`, `organisation` and `organisationUrl`). A request is mailed to the approved managers of that organisation and to the administrators (once each, never the requester), the decision to the requester, each with an inbox item; a role change and "no manager" are inbox items only. All are in the notification category `membership`, which a person can switch off. The links point to screens that arrive in a later release.
