M7 sprint 2: who may do what to a service, and the identity of a service. Three groups now act on one service. The approved **members** of its provider (the organisation that owns it) change its name, licence, stage, description, URL, `sameAs` links, contact point, icon and consortia; the approved **managers** of the provider also change its **slug** and its **provider** (a manager can move a service only to an organisation they manage too); administrators can change everything. **Reviewers and administrators alone** make a service public or private again. New internal API routes: `PATCH /api/internal/services/{id}` (a partial body; `null` clears an optional field; a request that names a field the caller may not write is refused whole with a 403 that names the fields and never their values, and nothing is written), `PUT` and `DELETE /services/{id}/icon` (a denied caller stores no file) and `PUT /services/{id}/publication` with `{ "public": true | false }`. `POST /services` is now open to every user, and the service requires the caller to be an administrator or an approved member of the provider they choose (otherwise 403). `GET /services/{id}` returns `editableFields` and `allowedActions` for the caller, so a screen can draw its form and buttons from what the server will accept. A rename keeps the old slug working (it still resolves and answers the current record with `redirectedFrom`) and the old slug can never be given to another service; two renames to one slug at once, or a rename and a create, give one winner and a 409 to the other. The setting `allowPublic` is now effective for publication: while it is off, making a service public is refused with a 409 (making it private is always allowed). The contact point of a service is shown to administrators and the provider's members always and to other signed-in people while `exposeContactPoint` is on. The delegated rights are read from the provider's memberships at every request, so removing or demoting a person takes effect at their next call. Access tokens need the scopes `registry.services.service.edit`, `…edit-identity` or `…publish` as well as `…service.read` for these routes. New permissions: `registry.services.service.create`, `.publish`, and three scoped to a service (`.edit`, `.edit-identity`, `.read-contact`); the roles `user` and `reviewer` get `create` and `publish` respectively. The audit trail records `registry.service.updated@1` (field names and the group that was used, never a value) and the critical `…slugChanged@1`, `…published@1` and `…unpublished@1`. The shared image block of the organisation logo now lives in the UI kit (`ImageBlock`) with unchanged behaviour. Nothing is public yet; the public reads follow in a later release.
