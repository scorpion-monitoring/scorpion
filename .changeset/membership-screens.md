People can find an organisation and ask to join it in the browser, organisation managers look after their own organisation on one page, and administrators decide membership requests on a page of their own. Together with the earlier changes of this release this completes the organisation and membership features of `registry.organisations`.

For everyone who is signed in: **Account → Organisations** (`/account/organisations`) lists your own memberships with a badge in words ("Pending", "Member", "Manager", "Rejected", "Left") and a button to withdraw or leave, and a search for organisations (by name or abbreviation, with a type filter, five per page, the number of members and your own status on each row, and a button to ask to join where that can work). The organisation page now links an administrator to its requests and a manager to their own page.

For organisation managers: when you manage an organisation the same page shows a section **My organisations** (and only then; nobody else sees it). Per organisation it has the requests waiting for a decision (approve, or reject after a short confirm), the members with the actions the server allows (make manager, make member, remove after a confirm that says what removal means), and the edit form for the description, website, `sameAs` links, ROR id, contact point and logo. Type, abbreviation and name are shown as text with the line "Only an administrator can change these; ask one." You cannot decide on your own request, change your own role or remove yourself, and only an administrator removes a manager; the page says so where a button is missing. A page that is out of date shows the refusal in words and reloads.

For administrators: **Administration → Membership requests** (`/admin/memberships`, the page the request mail and inbox item link to) lists the requests of every organisation, oldest first, with filters by state, organisation and type, and approve, reject, promote, demote and remove. Your own request has no approve or reject button and says why. The dashboard shows a card "Membership requests" with the number waiting, for administrators and managers, only while there is at least one; a person without requests to decide sees no card.

For operators: no new setting, permission, route or migration, and no new dependency. The request mail links to these pages and the decision mail to the organisation page, all under the instance's base path. Every screen is in English and German, passes an automated accessibility check in both themes and can be used with the keyboard alone. The module's README lists the screens, the delegation and field rules, the Schema.org mapping, how another module adds an organisation type or a usage check, and how a profile includes the module. `docs/security/authorization.md` has the field table of the membership.
