M9 sprint 1: the `kpi.framework` module, in the `full` profile only (the `registry` and `core-only` images do not change). It adds six tables with the prefix `kpif_` (indicators, the means of measurement and tools of an indicator, KPI sets, the indicators of a set with their necessity, and the evaluation of a set) that a migration creates at start. There are no pages and no API routes yet; they follow in the next releases. Indicators follow the Research Infrastructure Metrics Ontology (RIMO 0.2.0). Four new vocabularies appear under Settings: `kpi.unit` (count, percent, second, byte, request, user, publication, citation), `kpi.measured-by` and `kpi.automation-tool` (empty: add your own), and `kpi.service-category` (the 16 service categories of RIMO). A term of `thematic-category`, `necessity` or `aggregate` that an indicator, a KPI set or an evaluation uses can no longer be deleted, only deactivated. The new setting `iriBase` of `kpi.framework` (an `http(s)` URL, or empty) is the base of the stable identifiers of indicators and KPI sets: set it before you share any identifier, because changing it later changes all of them. The audit trail records `kpi.indicator.changed@1` and `kpi.set.changed@1` when `core.audit` is in the profile.
