'scorpion': minor

The managers of an organisation can now edit its descriptive fields and logo, and administrators get an editor in the browser. The organisation has a page of its own.

For administrators: an organisation manager can change the description, website, `sameAs` links, ROR id, contact point and logo of **their own organisation** and nothing else. Only an administrator can change an organisation's type, abbreviation and name, create an organisation or delete one; a manager who asks for any of those is refused as a whole and nothing is saved. A manager's edit applies at once (there is no review step), and `core.audit` logs it as a security-critical entry that names the manager and the changed fields (never the values), so it shows with the admin channel switched off. A demoted or removed manager loses the right at the next request. The contact point is still the organisation's own role address, never used as a recipient of any mail. New permission: the scoped `registry.organisations.organisation.edit` (held by administrators everywhere and by an organisation's managers for that organisation; it appears in the token scopes). New screens: **Administration → Organisations** lists organisations (search, type filter, pages), creates and edits them with every field and the logo, and deletes one after a confirm that says the memberships and the logo go with it; a type that another module still refers to is shown in words. `/organisations/{id}` shows an organisation to every signed-in person, with the type label from the registry, its links, the contact point for those who may see it, the member count, your own membership with the button that fits, and the member list when you may see it. The page carries the organisation as Schema.org JSON-LD in the document head, written on the server from the same profile route and escaped so organisation text cannot break out of it. The screens are in English and German.

For API users: `PATCH /organisations/{id}`, `PUT /organisations/{id}/logo` and `DELETE /organisations/{id}/logo` now accept every signed-in person at the route and decide in the service: `403` for anyone who may not edit that organisation, `403` "These fields can only be changed by an administrator: name." for a manager who names `type`, `abbreviation` or `name`, and `404` before `403` for an unknown organisation. `POST` and `DELETE /organisations` stay administrator-only. `GET /organisations/{id}` and every answer that returns an organisation now include `editableFields` (every field for an administrator, the descriptive ones and the logo for a manager of that organisation, an empty list for everybody else), which is what the screens draw their forms from. The event `registry.organisation.updated@1` carries `by: admin | manager`.
