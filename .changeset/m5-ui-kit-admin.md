Administrators can now manage users, roles and settings in the browser (M5, third part). **Administration → Users**: every account with a filter by status, a search and a sort, the accounts waiting for approval (the page the notification mail links to, `/admin/users/pending`), and one account's page: its roles (give and take away), its open access tokens (revoke), ending all its sessions, and deactivating it. Ending every session of everybody is on the list page, behind a confirm. **Administration → Roles** shows each role's permissions grouped by module and edits the permissions of every role except Admin. **Administration → Settings** has a form for the settings of every module (drawn from the module's own description of its settings, saved with the version that was read), the branding with logo upload and the legal texts, the secrets (names and when they were set; a value is never shown and cannot be read back) and the vocabularies (add, relabel in English and German, reorder, deactivate). The section appears in the navigation only for a person who holds the permission of each page.

New API routes (all internal): `GET /users` (query `status`, `q`, `sort`, `dir`, `page`, `pageSize`; permission `core.identity.user.read`), `GET /users/{id}`, `GET /users/{id}/roles` (also needs `core.authz.role.read`), `GET /users/{id}/tokens` (`core.identity.token.manage-any`, a session), `POST /users/{id}/deactivate` (`core.identity.user.deactivate`; audited) and `PUT /roles/{key}/permissions` (`core.authz.role.manage`; audited; Admin cannot be edited). Two new permissions, `core.identity.user.read` and `core.identity.user.deactivate`, are held by Admin only. A new event `identity.user.deactivated@1` is recorded by the audit trail.

Behaviour changes for operators: an account has a new status, `deactivated` (migration `0009`). A deactivated account cannot sign in, its sessions end in the same transaction, its access tokens stop working (a verified token may still be accepted for its short cache time), and the cleanup job does not delete it; there is no route to reactivate one yet. "The last Admin" now means the last Admin who can sign in: a deactivated Admin does not count, and the last active Admin can be neither removed from the role nor deactivated. Settings forms show the title and description of a field when its schema gives one (`.meta({ title, description, group, widget })`); the schemas of the shipped modules now do. German texts for the new screens are included. New runtime dependency: `echarts` (charts, loaded only on a page that draws one). The light and dark themes now use the colours of the UI/UX style guide (light-mode primary, error, success and warning are darkened so text meets WCAG AA; see `docs/ui-style-guide.md`), with a visible focus ring on every control.
