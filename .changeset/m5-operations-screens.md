The operations screens (M5, fourth part, first half). Administrators get three pages in the Administration menu: **Logs** (the audit trail with filters for method, user, endpoint, action, outcome, source and days, "Load more", one entry with its redacted body, and a CSV download that is itself recorded), **System** (the event outbox with a button that puts one dead delivery back in the queue, the retention numbers, and the history of job runs with the counts each job returned) and **Notification status** (counts, the error codes of the last week, a banner when the mail transport is `none`, the delivery list without bodies or addresses, a requeue for one dead mail and a test-mail button).

New API: `GET /system/job-runs` (permission `core.audit.system.read`; list envelope, newest first; filters `jobName` and `status`), `GET /permissions` (every declared permission with its module and description; `core.authz.role.read`) and `GET /account/permissions` (the caller's own permissions, limited to the scopes of an access token; new permission `core.authz.account.read`, held by the role `user`). The log entries returned by `GET /audit` and `GET /audit/{id}` carry `userName`: the name the account has now, `null` for a purged account. `GET /audit` (the list) is **no longer an audit entry itself**, because the Logs page pages it; opening one entry and the CSV export still are. The roles page and the access-token form now offer what these routes list, with descriptions, instead of guessing.

Changed behaviour: a browser whose sign-in at an OpenID provider fails (account waiting for approval, an old link, a provider that refused or is down) is redirected to `/login?error=<code>` with a code from a fixed list and sees a message in its language, instead of a page of JSON; a client that does not ask for `text/html` still gets the problem answer (ADR-0029). The settings forms store only the values that differ from the default, so a later change of a default reaches instances that saved the form earlier; existing stored settings are untouched. Removing an item of a list in a form asks first. The sign-in and registration pages say whom to ask when a registration was declined (the contact address of the branding settings).

New optional variable for the web process: `API_TIMEOUT_MS` (default 30000, `0` for none): how long a call of the API may be silent before the proxy answers 504 (event streams are exempt). No new runtime dependency.
