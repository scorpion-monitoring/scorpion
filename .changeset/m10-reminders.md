The `full` profile now sends a monthly reporting reminder. A daily job (`kpi.ingestion.reporting-reminder`, 06:00 UTC) acts on one day of the month, the 6th by default, and mails the approved members of each provider the services of that provider that still have no value for a mandatory indicator of the previous month (a service with no KPI set has nothing missing; a KPI the service has dropped is not asked for). Only people who have an active account and an address and may report for the service are mailed, and a person who switched the `reminders` category off in their notification preferences is not. A provider is reminded once per month, even if the job runs again, is retried or runs on two nodes (a new table, `kpii_reminder_sent`, is created by a migration at the next start). The mail lists the services and indicators (at most 20 of each, then a count), never a value, and links to the Data Submission page on the first service and that month; it is in English and German and takes the instance name and sender from the settings. Two new settings on the module's settings page: `reminders.enabled` (default on) and `reminders.dayOfMonth` (1 to 28, default 6, counted in UTC; a value outside the range is refused). The template `kpi.reporting-reminder` moved from `core.notifications` to `kpi.ingestion` with the same key: a profile without `kpi.ingestion` (`core-only`, `registry`) no longer registers it and no longer lists the `reminders` category on the notification preferences page. There is no way to run the reminder on demand: the job page lists runs only. `kpi.ingestion` now depends on `core.identity`, `core.settings`, `core.notifications` and `registry.organisations`, which every profile that has it already contains. The public API `/api/v1` is unchanged.
