M7 sprint 6: the service page, editing, publication and the public pages. **`/services/:slug`** (every signed-in person; the catalogue and the end of the create wizard now link to it) shows a service in words: its icon, name, short name, stage and licence by name, description, web address and further links, who operates it and what it is part of (each links to its organisation page), the contact point only where the API returned it, and a "Public" or "Private" badge. An id or an old short name in the address goes on to the current one. **Editing:** a person the server allows to edit gets an Edit button and a form drawn from what the server says they may change, never from a role. A member changes the name, licence, stage, description, links, contact point, icon and groups, and sees the short name and the owner as fixed text with "Only a manager of the provider or an administrator can change these."; a manager of the provider also changes the short name (the old address keeps working, and the page says so) and moves the service to another organisation they manage; an administrator changes everything. A save sends only what changed; a page that is out of date meets a 403 in words and keeps what was typed; a short name that is taken is shown on its field. **Publication:** Reviewers and administrators get Make public and Make private, each with a confirmation that says what it means (the contact point is never public); with `service.allowPublic` off, making a service public is refused and the page says why. **Two public pages, readable without signing in:** `/public/services/:slug` and `/public/organisations/:id`. They show only what a Reviewer made public (the pinned public record, never the contact point), carry the Schema.org block in their head, link the operator and the groups to their public pages, and draw nothing from who is looking. A service that is not public, an organisation no public service names, and every address while `service.allowPublic` is off give the same "page does not exist" answer as an unknown address; unpublishing takes effect at the next request (a shared cache may show the page for up to a minute, as for the public routes). To switch every public page and public route off at once, turn off `service.allowPublic` on the settings page of the services module; services already marked public are served again when it is turned back on. The page list for anonymous visitors now includes the two public pages (the check that lists them in `PUBLIC_PAGES` is updated). **API:** no route was added or changed. **For module authors:** a page's `load` receives `redirect(path)` (a path below the base path) and throws SvelteKit's redirect; the edit form uses the same widgets and field helpers as the create wizard.
