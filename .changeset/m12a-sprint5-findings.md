Findings of the blocking items (M12a). **Lists:** the page buttons and the page-size choice of every list are disabled while a new page is loading, so "Next" pressed during a search no longer asks for the page of the old query. **Tokens:** a permission can now declare in its module manifest which other permission's scope implies it (`impliedBy`); the token of a provider's member or manager can change a service (`PATCH /services/{id}`) with the new scope `registry.services.service.write`, which the role `user` holds and the token form offers, and the owner still has to be allowed to change that service. The scope of the public write of measurements works the same way now. **Audit trail:** `POST /auth/logout` is an entry (caller, client address, outcome); the entry of a successful sign-in has the client address in its `ip` column, which the retention cuts after `ipTruncateAfterDays`; a wrong password at a re-authentication or a password change is counted like a failed sign-in (`context` on `identity.login.failed@1`); and the failed sign-ins of names that are no account are counted one by one only up to `loginThrottle.auditMaxUnknownNames` (default 200) per window, the rest together, so a spray of invented names cannot grow the table. A new migration changes `identity_login_failure`; nobody is locked out.
